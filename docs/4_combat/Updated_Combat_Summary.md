# Summaries
## Approach & Combat Stance
Stance represents the approach, tactic and intent of a combatant in that round. Each person selects a stance each combat round, in the 1st round based on lowest AGL declaring 1st. In subsequent rounds, reverse momentum order.
- 1st Turn Momentum modifier -> when calculating momentum in the 1st turn use this modifier
- Conditions -> requirements to allow the use of the Stance
- Roll Modifier is applied to the Opposed Roll
- Limits/Benefits -> Limitations on reactions or moves, and modifiers on Knock-Down
- Outcome is a action/condition on the result of the rolls
	- Next Round is an effect which carries over into next round

[[Approach_Stances]]

## Momentum
- Compare ACTION speeds
	- + Stance/Action modifiers
	- - 1 for each Medium or Serious Flesh wound
	- + 1 for martial experience
- Ties on Luck, then Actor level then to Player Actors

(Action speed is AGL-2 + Composure-2)
## Opposed Rolls

Opposed rolls -> Skill + Modifiers + Reaction outcomes - Resistance = net successes

In reverse momentum order, each Actor can choose to use dice patterns to enhance their opposed roll.

Compare results, including any boost modifiers
- Net Highest = winner
- Tie = resolved on Momentum

Apply the results of the opposed roll, update momentum, and then move on to resolve any additional reactions in reverse momentum order. The momentum could have switched from the start of the round.

## Dice Patterns - reading the pool
The dice pool results contain dice patterns other than how many successes are achieved. Look for doubles, triples, quads to enable boosts, reactions or additional damage. Dice patterns apply to both opponent's dice pools.

Each pattern can only be <u>used once</u>
- Successes (use only sixes)
- Reactions dice patterns depending on the weapon type, and that are equal or less than AGL
- Additional Damage dice patterns depending on the weapon type, and equal or less than 5 

In some cases using pattern greater than the required can deliver additional results. E.g. using a 'triple' when only a 'double'+ is required.

In addition information can be generated from the pool, this doesn't require the pattern to be un-used, and <u>doesn't</u> use the pattern.
- Hit Location
- Results of Fumbles
- Mount actions

### Melee Dice Patterns
- All participants get reaction opportunities with <u>dice patterns</u> in their Base dice (Bd) pool.
- Boosts & Reactions require patterns determined by their weapon, and that <u>are at or under their AGL</u> (reduced by any perm AGL conditions). 
- Additional Wound points require patterns determined by their weapon, and that are at or under their PHY (reduced by any perm PHY conditions)

Multiple dice patterns can be used to create multiple reactions. Dice patterns can be to split to create multiple smaller patterns i.e. a quad can be two pairs.

Dice can only be used <u>once</u> during a round for the following;
- Sixes used to oppose rolls
- Patterns at or under <u>PHY</u> can be used for <u>Additional Wounds</u>
- Patterns at or under <u>AGL</u> can be used for <u>Reactions</u>, the size of the pattern depends on the weapon type
	- ==Trained & accomplished== fighters can use patterns at ≤AGL+1
	- ==Expert== fighters can use patterns at up to ≤AGL+2
- Doubles+ of fives or less can be used as additions to come Reactions outcome results e.g. Double Feint
- Smaller patterns can be combined to create Larger patterns e.g. two 'Doubles' can be used to count as a 'Triple'

<u>Having a large Resistance dice</u> (Rd) <u>pool</u> will reduce the combatant's AGL for generating dice patterns
- Rd pool of 5 or more Rd dice reduce their AGL by 1 
- Rd pool of 10 or more dice reduce their AGL by 2 
- Rd pool are used as priority before any other uses
### Common Melee Boosts & Additional Damage
Common melee reactions available to all Actors, and most reactions are applicable to the winner or loser of a contest. Exceptions listed.

Choose & includes any boost reactions into the main opposed roll resolution, and apply immediately.
Once the main roll has been resolved, the additional reactions can be chosen and applied in a reverse momentum sequential order, each combatant choosing one reaction, back and forth until all reactions have been used or waived.

Each Boost, Additional Damage or Reaction can only <u>be used ONCE per round.</u>

### Knocked-Back, knocked-Down, Knocked-Over, Knocked-Flat, Knocked-Out
- <u>Knock-down</u> is Strength + successes + modifiers from stance or reactions - Rd pool modifier
- <u>Knock-down defense</u> is <u>average</u> of defender's Phy and Agl (round down) + modifiers (stance, reactions, location) - Rd pool modifier

- Rd Pool Modifier = Check the highest, widest dice pool pattern;
	- If even +1 (including if zero Rd)
	- If odd -1

- If attackers knock-down is greater or equal to defender's knock-down defense, then move 1 step down the ladder.
- If the attacker's result is greater than or equal to <u>triple</u> (≥3X), then move 2 steps
- If the attacker's result is greater or equal to <u>sixfold</u> (≥6X), then move 3 steps
- If the attacker's result is greater or equal to <u>eightfold</u> (≥8X), then move to Knocked-Out regardless of the starting place.

- If Knock-down defense is ≤0 calculate at 1

#### Knocked-Back Ladder

- Any knock-back down the ladder includes moving a single distance apart.
	1. **Knocked-back** (reeling)
		1. 2Rd
		3. No other penalties
	2. **Knocked-down** (staggered)
		1. 4Rd
		3. No movement reactions in the turn moved into knock-down
	3. **Knocked-over** (floored)
		1. 6Rd
		2. No movement reactions or counterattack reactions
		3. Take 1 wound pt, not blocked by Armor
	4. **Knocked-flat** (winded)
		1. 6Rd
		2. Take <u>one</u> 1 wound point injury, not blocked by Armor
		3. No movement or any reactions
	5. **Knocked-out** (KO) (only use if knocked-out from normal or knocked-back, as a result of one move. i.e. isn't used if knocked back from Knocked-down)
		1. Knocked-out
		3. Take <u>two</u> 1 wound pt injuries, not blocked by Armor

- Recovering from knock-back 
	- One step recovered per turn (the penalty applied is in part the distraction to recover)
	- By specific Reactions and Dramatic Actions
		- Recover Footing
		- Roll
### Disadvantage
- Independent from knock-back
- A count of additional Rd applied to subsequent rounds as a result of a prior actions, if any
- Lasts as long as the prior action is in effect (mostly one turn)

# Weapons
Weapons have a requirement to wield, weight and an effective reach. Some weapons can be used in different ways, and their weight and reach change depending on the Grip.

Using a weapon at a range which isn't the effective, means that capability isn't available

A Limited Damage weapon does 1 Wound pt for 1 or 2 net successes, 3 or more successes count fully. 
- Winning with 2 net successes causes 1 wound + any wound modifiers. 
- Winning with 3 net successes causes 3 wounds + any wound modifiers.

#### Grapple Ladder
Each combatant can be on the ladder at the same time - both parties can be held and attempting to restrain the other.

1. **Unheld**
	1. No effect
2. **Held**
	1. Partial hold which attracts a 2Rd disadvantage
	2. Both parties can be Held
	3. Holding requires 1+ free hand
3. **Restrained**/Lock
	1. Firmly held with a 4Rd disadvantage
4. **Overpowered**
	1. Held with a 4Rd disadvantage
	2. And at a minimum Knocked-Back effect (a further 2Rd)
	3. If also Actor who is overpowered is also holding their opponent, reduce by current grapple status on opponent by 1 step (this can mean the hold is released)
5. **Strangled**
	1. If Chest or Head location locked
	2. Knocked-out
	3. Option to complete the job...

Changes in position on the ladder only come as a result of a Stance or Reaction.
