# Brawls & Wrestling


- Goals
	- Knock-out -> via bruising damage
	- Restrain -> hold, unbroken
	- Inflict damage -> improvised weapon, lucky strike
	- Knock-down to do something else, e.g. escape -> 

- Brawl (Phy) -> hit or grab
- Athletics (Agl) -> grab or evade
- Strength & Stamina (Phy) -> grab or break

### Bruising hits
- In general Brawling inflicts bruising 1pt wounds which can be recovered in 1 day
- But, the larger wound slots are filled with repeated hits, using O rather than X to remember their recovery time is 1 day rather then a week
- It is still possible to be downed through number of hits
- Brawling is a Light weapon type a Quad (4+) will add one additional damage point of full damage, and cause that wound to be a full wound

### Grab & Grapple
- Choose which skill to be opposed roll - Athletics or S&S or Brawl
- Winner has option to have a **hold**, and momentum
- When Held +2Rd
	- S&S can be used to break free
- From a hold, option to **restrain** with S&S
	- Opposed roll, either breaks the hold or restrained
- From a hold, option to **throw** with Athletics or S&S
	- This breaks the hold
- Restrained is 4Rd
- Subsequent rounds add +2Rd to the restrained hold

# Improvised Weapons
> Generally something which comes to hand in a fight, rather than a prepared weapon
- Uses whichever skill makes most sense
	- Strike for a more substantial item -> flying pan
	- Brawl for something that enhances your fist/foot -> bottle, rock
	- Throw/Ranged for something you throw -> rock, bottle, can
- Improv weapons can be light or medium weapons 
- And improv weapons do regular rather than bruising damage

### Automatic Fire
- Roll additional Drama Dice representing the effect of the extra shots
- Two successes in the Drama pool equal a second wound, which causes the full Flesh damage of the shot, less any defensive armor saves. This would mean the defender rolling additionally for their armor against this shot. No additional damage is done as a result of successes through Grit for the subsequent hit.
- Two '1s' in the Drama pool indicates a jam, out of ammo or mis-fire

| :--- | :--- |:----|
| Semi Auto Fire | +2 Dd | +1 Phy |
| Full Auto Fire | +4 Dd | +2 Phy |

## Explosives
- Explosive rating determines the power and impact of the explosion
	- 1 stick of dynamite has power 1
	- a bundle (6) sticks have power 3
	- a modern grenade has power 6
- Knock down happens regardless of hit/number of success, just being in the explosion area causes a potential knock-down
	- Knock-down is Power + successes + range modifier
	- Power is reduced by range
	- Deafness
- Number of hits caused is >1 [TBD could be a natural product of large rolls and high type]
- Protection from blast based on position

### Reaction Actions in Melee
- Successes can be used to add an additional action(s)
- This applies to **all/both parties** in the melee
- Reactions are called in reverse momentum order, alternating if there are more than one reaction action to be chosen per person
- No move stances/actions possible when knocked-down (double or triple disadvantaged)

- **Ripost**/counter attack/secondary attack
	- Max 1pt wound, no knock-down, can be blocked by armor
	- unless modifier by a Drama pt, to push the damage up by 1 per Drama point spent
	- No ripost/counter/secondary reaction attack is possible with a weapon that has a reach modifier of 4Rd (it is out of its effective range)
	
- **Change range** (reactively - so you can counter or keep someone at distance)
	- Move into Up Close, or out from Melee to Distanced
- If loser **Defend**
	- Convert successes into +1Ad per success
- If winner **Bash** (kick, trip, barge, weapon or shield bash)
	- adds 2 to a knock-down test
- If using the Feint attack, **Steal Momentum**
- If Up Close, possible to **Grab/hold**
- If Up Close, and already grabbed, then possible **Throw**
- If at Distance, **Keep at Distance**
- **Quick Action**
	- Draw a short weapon, grab something

## Ranged Boosts & Reactions
- Trained or Expert skills can generate reactions
- **Trip Dice patterns** based on Agility
	- Agility of 3 = a success per trip (of 3, 2, 1)
	- Agility of 4 = a success per trip (of 4,3,2,1)
	- Agility of 5+ = a success per trip (of 5,4,3,2,1)
- Based on the single widest result where the number is equal or lower than the Agility
- Multiple reactions are possible, when width of one number allows
> e.g. AGL4 rolled 66444221 two successes + 1 reaction from the trip 4's
> or AGL3 with the same roll, would be 2 successes and no reactions - the width is greater than AGL and would need a quad in a 3 or lower
