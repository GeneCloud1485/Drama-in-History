# Weapons

1. ==Weapon 'weight' or 'lethality' translates net successes into wounds - old
	1. Unarmed 1-4 successes = 1(+)-3 or (successes-3), min of 1
	2. Limited 1-3 successes = 1(+)-2 or (successes-2), min of 1
	3. Light 1-2 successes = 1(+)-1 or (successes-1), min of 1
	4. Medium 1:1 success = 1(+) 
	5. High/Heavy = 2(+)
	6. Very Heavy = 3(+)
	7. Extreme = 4(+)

- Grip determines effective reach
- Parry when used outside of the effective reach -1Ad
- Weight+ weapons can do extra damage with more successes
