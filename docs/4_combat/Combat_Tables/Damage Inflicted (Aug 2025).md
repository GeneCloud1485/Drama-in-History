# Calculating Damage

1. Base damage = stagger value
2. + Weapon damage
	1. Unarmed = 0
	2. Limited = 0
	3. Light = 1
	4. Mid = 2
	5. Heavy = 3
	6. V Heavy = 4
	7. Extreme = 5
3. + Hit Location modifiers
	1. Triple ≥6's add wound pts for weight+ weapons
	2. Locations dice patterns can add addition wounds or effects
4. + Special Impacts bonuses
	1. ≥Pair of 5's or 6's can trigger special effects

- At least 1pt of wound must be inflicted to target's Flesh
