A Limited Damage weapon does 1 Wound pt for 1 or 2 net successes, 3 or more successes count fully.  
- Winning with 1 or 2 net successes causes 1 wound + any wound modifiers. 
- Winning with 3 net successes causes 3 wounds + any wound modifiers.
