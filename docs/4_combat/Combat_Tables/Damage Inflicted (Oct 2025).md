# Calculating Damage

1. Wound damage is
	1. + Base damage
	2. + Rolled 6's
	3. + Weapon threat damage
		1. + Weapon's <u>Threat</u> when at effective reach, (-1)Threat if not at effective reach.
			1. Unarmed = -2
			2. limited = -1
			3. Light = 0
			4. Mid = 1
			5. Heavy = 2
			6. Extreme = 3
2. + Hit Location determines special outcomes/modifiers
	1. ≥Triple of location number can trigger special effects

 
**Any ≥triple 6's for loser = Parry opportunity**

==Impact = net number of successes==

Impact is generated
- At least 1pt of wound must be inflicted to target's Flesh
- Impact = wound pts after dodge and parry, but before armor TBD - as Ad rolls likely combined for simplicity 
- Unarmed impact = Attacker's PHY + Special modifiers
