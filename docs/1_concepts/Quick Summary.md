# <u>Dice & Pools</u>
### Dice
- Base dice (Bd) -> successes on a roll of 6
- Resistance dice (Rd) -> negative successes on a roll of 1
![[Pasted image 20231202151026.png]]
### Build & Roll a Pool
- Build a pool from Base and Resistance dice
- Add successes and negative success together to get net success
- If 1 or more the roll is successful
- The greater the number, the more successful the outcome
- If the net result is negative, the more unsuccessful the outcome
# <u>Attributes </u>
![[Pasted image 20231016192534.png|500]]
- Base attributes on a scale up to 6
- Each attribute is included in the calculation of skills
- Or, on occasion are tested directly
# <u>Status</u> (Currencies)
> HP or Sanity equivalent

![[Pasted image 20231016192508.png]]
- Scale of 3-6
- Determines the number of 'wounds' that can be taken to this particular status
- Being out of available slots to take a wound in any given status = KO'ed
-> You can KO'ed for being 'Out of Luck' just as you can for taking a number of physical wounds
- A 1pt wound is recovered after 1 days rest
- 2pt wounds take a week
- 3 pt wounds take a month
- 4pt wound will KO the character and recovery takes 3 months recovery time
## Flesh/Body
- Physical and always represents a wound or damage of some kind
## Luck
- Avoiding bad things happening, or used to press your luck for advantage
## Fortitude
- Underlying mental stability
## Composure/Nerve
- Confidence, nerve
# <u>Conditions</u>
> Permanent, status or temporary conditions whose effects modify your chance of success

![[Pasted image 20231016192648.png|200]]
- Represented as resistance dice Rd
- Permanent Conditions are applied only to the attribute, and calculated into the skill
- Status Conditions result from wounds, and last only as long as the wound exists. The Condition impacts all rolls.
	- A 1pt condition are recovered after a day
	- A 2pt condition recovery takes a week
- Temp Conditions impact all rolls, while they last
# <u>Actor Level & Drama Points</u>
![[Pasted image 20231016192753.png|300]]
- Actor Level is a form of progression, and reflects how famous an actor is
- Actor's get Drama Points depending on their actor level, or due to acceptance of Scars
- Drama points are a finite currency, which recover after the completion of a Story or Season
### Use of Drama Points
- Spending a Drama Point can trigger a Dramatic Advantage
	- If this is aligned with the intent of the Advantage then 3Bd can be added
	- If this is a more general 'dramatic' moment, add +2Bd, otherwise it can be used to get +1Bd
- Drama Points can be used to reduce a 'wound' by a level per point spent, there needs to be an available slot
# <u>Common Skills</u>
- Common skills cover the majority of normal activities
	- Inexperienced = no skill points allocated
	- Untrained = skill points allocated 
	- Trained = specific training
	- Expert/Mastery = specific extended training
- They can be improved with experience/age adding Bd
- The Skill is calculated as base Attribute + modifier
- And the skill can receive resistance Rd as a result of Permanent Conditions
- The Skill is written as XBd + YRd
- A common skill can't be higher than 6Bd
![[Pasted image 20231016200318.png|300]]
# <u>Trained or Mastered Skills</u>
- Specific skills are earned through experience or backgrounds
- They are extensions to Common Skills, and the Bd & Rd from the common skill are counted
- Training in a skill provides +2Bd, and will usually narrow the common skills scope of application
- Mastery in a skill provides +4Bd
![[Pasted image 20231016200210.png|500]]
# <u>Age & Backgrounds</u>
- Each band represents an age range, and tracks the experiences gained during that period
- Backgrounds indicate the number of skill points available, and should steer the selection of skills which are advanced during that period.
- In some cases, backgrounds can stand in for a specific skills at a reduced rate
![[Pasted image 20231016194243.png|400]]

# <u>Bonds & Beliefs</u>
- Represent people, beliefs and commitments made
- Provide guidance for role play
- Can be used to modify a pool when a bond or belief is central to the test
# <u>Scars & Dramatic Advantages</u>
- A scar reflects major, and damaging memories which add +1Rd in any situation which touches on the context of the Scar.
- Dramatic Advantages can be called upon in appropriate situations, they cost 1 Drama Point to activate and add +3Bd
