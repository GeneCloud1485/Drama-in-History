
==<u>Back to 5 & 6 for successes</u>
<u>And 1 & 2 for negative success</u>==

> generate more results rather than 0 failures
- Meaning more wounds >1 = more interesting and quicker combat

Status pools = Stat + 1 + age_mod (to a min of 1)

Any single wound which matches the Stat pool = KO

Zero in the stat pool is 'mortal' position

Any ≥2 pt hit causes a wound
- 2 = 1Rd
- 3 = 2Rd
- 4 = 3Rd
And each of these wounds have a healing time

General status pool recovers at 2 pts per day

The status pool Rd set against specific skills
